Hi, I'm Sarah

I like understanding how systems work,

why people actually use them,

and how to make that experience better.

That instinct led me to study Computer Science and Business Administration — I see them less as separate disciplines than as parts of the same craft: one gives an idea structure, the other gives it direction.

I'm aiming toward Associate Product Manager roles, and I like building things myself along the way — this site included.

I'm Sarah, a Computer Science and Business Administration student at USC's Viterbi School of Engineering, after a strong first year at Northeastern. I like working at the intersection of technical execution and product thinking — leading a 6-person cross-functional team as PM for an AI companion app, coordinating a backend data migration for an iOS app with 800+ users, and researching how cities and small businesses actually adopt AI.

What I care about

That last thread runs through a lot of what I do. Through Northeastern's Khoury College, I studied Oakland's citywide Microsoft Copilot deployment, analyzing real interview data from city officials to inform how AI prompting should be taught. And through the Khoury AI Clinic, I advised a small business — closed since COVID — on AI tools and automated workflows; their Instagram output went from 22 posts over four years to 11 posts in two months, including their first farmers market appearance since reopening.

Outside of work

Right now I'm building Floating Notebook, a Chrome extension with a floating note-taking panel that overlays any webpage — plus this site, which is its own ongoing experiment in interaction design. I'm aiming toward Associate Product Manager roles, and I like building things myself along the way.
